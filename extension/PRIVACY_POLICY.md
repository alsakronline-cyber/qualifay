# Privacy Policy for LeadList

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how LeadList ("we", "our", or "the extension") handles information when you use our Chrome Extension.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
LeadList is designed to help you extract contact details (such as names, job titles, companies, locations, email addresses, and phone numbers) from LinkedIn profiles and search pages that you actively visit.

**All extracted data is stored 100% locally on your device** using Chrome's secure local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). 
* **We do not collect, store, or transmit your data to any external servers.**
* We do not have access to your database, your scraped leads, or your browsing activity.

## 2. Permissions Justification
To perform its core functions, LeadList requests the following permissions:
* `storage`: To save your scraped leads on your computer so you can access and manage them later.
* `host_permissions` (*://*.linkedin.com/*): To allow the extension to run and inject its sidebar panel on LinkedIn pages.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Access
* **No Data Sharing:** Because all data remains locally on your device, we do not share, sell, trade, or transfer any user information to third parties.
* **No Ads or Tracking:** The extension contains no tracking pixels, analytics engines, or third-party ads.

## 4. Security
Your data is protected by Chrome's built-in application sandboxing and secure local storage mechanisms. We recommend keeping your Chrome browser up to date to ensure the highest security standards.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated locally within the extension files and in the Web Store listing.
